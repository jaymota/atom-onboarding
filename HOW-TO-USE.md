# How to use your onboarding platform

Your platform lives at **onboarding.atomconsulting.in**.

- **Clients** see the intake form (the home page), plus any private quote or details links you send them.
- **You** use the admin area at **onboarding.atomconsulting.in/admin** (or the faint “Team login” link at the bottom of the form).

---

## 1. Logging in

1. Go to **onboarding.atomconsulting.in/admin**.
2. Type your PIN and press **Unlock**.
3. **First time on a new computer or phone?** We email a 6-digit code to jay@atomconsulting.in. Type it in once. After that, that browser only needs the PIN.
4. If you leave the screen alone for **30 minutes**, it locks itself. Just type your PIN again.
5. **Forgot your PIN?** Click **Forgot PIN?**. We email a code to jay@atomconsulting.in, and you choose a new PIN.

> Never share your PIN. Nobody from Atom or from the platform will ever ask for it.

---

## 2. A new enquiry comes in

When someone fills in the form, three things happen:

- You get an email: **“New enquiry: Brand (Name)”**.
- The client appears on your **Dashboard** with the status **Quote draft**.
- A **draft quote** is already prepared from their answers. It picks the modules, Easy/Medium/Hard and Milestone/Retainer, and suggests your fee.

Click **Open quote →** to start.

---

## 3. Making a quote (about 5 minutes)

The quote builder has **controls on the left** and the **quote on the right**. The quote on the right looks exactly like the PDF.

Work down the left-hand sections:

1. **Client & product**: who it's addressed to, the product, number of variants and the date.
2. **Where does the project start?** Choose either *Ideation & Rapid Prototyping* (the client needs us to figure out the product) or *Literature Review* (they already know what they want). Phases renumber themselves.
   - Tick **“Rapid prototyping is the main service”** for 10 prototypes instead of 3.
3. **Service modules**: switch modules on or off. Click **what's included** to see the checklist tasks behind each one (only you see this).
4. **Complexity & model**: Easy, Medium or Hard fills in the timeline. Milestone or Retainer changes the wording.
   - Tastings and pilot trials default to **3 and 3** for projects, and **2 and 2 per month** for retainers. Change the numbers if needed, and the wording updates everywhere.
5. **Fees**: the fee starts as a grey **suggestion**. Change it if you like, then tick **“I’ve confirmed this fee”**. You can't approve without this.
   - Milestone quotes show the payment tranches. The total must be 100%, and it turns red if not.
6. **Timeline**: change any week number if you need to.
7. **Scope items**: add your own items, or bring back removed ones.
8. **Potential additional costs**: switch rows on or off, or add your own.
9. **Fee assumptions**: tick or untick clauses, or add your own.

**To change any wording**, click it in the quote on the right. A small box opens: edit the text, then press **Save**. **Reset to template wording** brings back the original. Use `**double stars**` to make words bold.

**To remove a scope item**, hover over it in the quote and click the small ✕.

Everything **saves by itself**; watch for “All changes saved” at the top.

Handy buttons at the top:
- **Intake answers**: see everything the client told you.
- **Versions**: earlier approved versions. You can restore one.

### Approve, then share
1. Press **Approve**. The quote gets its number (e.g. `ATOM/Q/2026-27/001`) and the PDF is saved.
2. Three big buttons appear:
   - **Share with client**: check the email (you can edit the subject and message), then press **Send email**. They get the PDF plus a button to view and accept online. You can also **Copy link** or **Share on WhatsApp**.
   - **Edit**: go back and change things. You'll need to approve again.
   - **Download PDF**: saves `Atom_Quote_Brand_Date.pdf` to your computer.

You can't share a quote that isn't approved, so a draft can never go out by mistake.

---

## 4. What the client does

They open the link and see the quote, with a **Download PDF** button and two choices:

- **Accept quote**: they type their name and tick a box. You get an email 🎉 and a **draft MOU is created automatically**.
- **Request changes**: they write what they want changed. You get an email. Open the quote: their comment is shown at the top. Edit, **Approve**, then **Share** again. **The same link shows the new version.**

You also get an email the **first time they open** the quote. Links stop working after the validity period (30 days by default).

---

## 5. The MOU

Open it from the email, or from the client's page → **MOU → Open**. Follow the three steps on the left.

1. **Check the client details**: anything missing (like PAN) is highlighted in yellow.
   - Type it in yourself, **or** click **“Email the client a ‘complete your details’ link”**. You'll get an email when they fill it in.
2. **Review and approve**:
   - Click any text to edit it, just like the quote.
   - **Milestone MOUs** have some new clauses (Fees, Termination, Invoicing, Clause G) tagged **“NEW: please have your lawyer review”**. Only you see these tags; they don't appear in the PDF.
   - Then press **Approve MOU**.
3. **Sign with BoldSign (free)**:
   1. Click **Download the final MOU PDF**.
   2. Click **Open BoldSign** → **Send document** → upload the PDF.
   3. Add the client's signatory first, then yourself.
   4. Drag a **Signature** and a **Date signed** box onto each signature area on the last page, then click **Send**.
   5. Back on the platform, click **“I've sent it for signature”**.
   6. When both have signed, BoldSign emails you the finished PDF with its audit trail. Click **Upload the signed PDF** to store it. The client becomes **Fully signed**.

The MOU's **Effective Date** reads “Date of the last signature on this MOU”, so it's set by the signing itself.

---

## 6. The dashboard

- The numbers at the top: enquiries this month, things waiting on you, quotes out with clients, and signed projects.
- Use the search box and the status filter to find clients.
- **+ New quote**: for clients who didn't use the form, such as a phone enquiry.
- **Import Google Form CSV**: brings in old Google Form responses (instructions are in the window).

On a client's page you can:
- change the status, including **Project started** and **Lost / Not proceeding** (with a reason)
- edit their company details
- see everything that has happened in **Activity**

## 7. Settings

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Company** | Your address, GSTIN and PAN, titles, quote validity, GST rate |
| **Fees & prices** | Suggested fees for Easy, Medium and Hard; prices for extra tastings, flavours and trials; default counts |
| **Timeline presets** | The week ranges for Easy, Medium and Hard |
| **Default wording** | Change the standard wording of any scope item, clause or MOU paragraph, for **new** quotes |
| **Email & signing** | Sender details, a **Send a test email** button, and the BoldSign link |
| **Security** | Change your PIN, see and remove trusted devices, see login history, **Log out everywhere** |
